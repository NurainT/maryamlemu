The Summit

Your family is your greatest investment.
Don’t leave its future to chance.

The strongest homes are built with intention. How we love, communicate, handle conflict, raise our children and nurture our faith will shape not only our families, but the generations that come after us.

The Muslim Family Summit brings together trusted speakers, counsellors and teachers for three days of honest conversations and practical guidance on marriage, parenting, relationships, faith and family life.

Come as a couple. Come alone. Bring your teenager. Come ready to learn, reconnect and take home tools you can begin using immediately.

Because some things are too important to figure out as you go.

  • Marriage that survives the hard seasons
  • Raising children with deen and character
  • Communication, conflict and repair
  • Intimacy, honesty and trust
  • Healing from what you inherited
  • Guiding teenagers through a loud world
The Convener

Maryam Lemu

Maryam Lemu is a relationship expert with over three and a half decades of experience in marriage, public speaking and personal development. She has travelled globally facilitating seminars and retreats on marital and premarital topics, team building and effective communication.

Her 35-year marriage to her husband, Sa'id, has been a journey of self-discovery, spiritual growth and true partnership — the work that inspired the Maryam Lemu Marriage Academy and its certified premarital masterclass.

What to Expect

Three days. Three experiences. One purpose.

Day One · The Main Summit 14 November 2026

Everyone. Every speaker. One room.

A full-day experience for men, women and couples, bringing our entire international speaker line-up together under one roof.

Powerful conversations on marriage, family, faith, parenting, intimacy, communication and building homes that thrive.

This is the day everyone should experience.

Day Two · Men & Women, Separate Rooms 15 November 2026 · Morning

Some conversations need their own space.

Separate rooms for men and for women, plus an afternoon session for teenage girls.

Men only

An honest, no-judgement space to talk manhood, positive masculinity, Qawwamah, marriage, fatherhood, leadership and the private struggles men rarely get to discuss openly.

Women only

A warm, safe space for honest conversations and practical workshops around womanhood, marriage, emotional wellbeing, spirituality, intimacy and the personal issues women need room to speak about freely.

Teenage girls · Afternoon

For girls aged 14–19. A special afternoon with Yasmin Mogahed and our female speakers, created for the next generation of Muslim women.

Faith. Identity. Confidence. Spirituality. Womanhood. Relationships. And the pressures young Muslim women face today.

A conversation we want our daughters to be part of.

Day Three · The Private Experience 16 November 2026

Fewer people. Deeper conversations. Direct access.

This is where the Summit becomes personal. Two exclusive, small-group experiences created for conversations that simply cannot happen in a large room.

Men’s private session & dinner

An intimate workshop with Abdullah Oduro and Habeeb Akande exploring manhood, marriage, intimacy, leadership, responsibility and the realities men often struggle to speak about openly.

Go deeper. Ask your questions directly. Sit with the speakers, share a meal and have honest conversations in a private, judgement-free space.

Ladies’ private session & dinner

A warm, relaxed and intimate evening with our female speakers, created especially for women.

Go beyond the stage and into real conversation about marriage, womanhood, intimacy, relationships, faith and the issues closest to your heart. Ask your questions directly, hear from the speakers personally, share a beautiful dinner and enjoy the freedom of a safe, women-only space.

Small groups. Real conversations. Direct access to the speakers. Very limited seats.
